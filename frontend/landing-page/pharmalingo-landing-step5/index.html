OUR STORY

Made for the moments when words matter.

PharmaLingo is an independently developed translation tool inspired by real pharmacy workflows. The application is currently being tested internally with a small pharmacy team as features and usability are refined.

PharmaLingo is a communication aid, not a substitute for professional medical interpretation or verification of clinical instructions. Do not enter identifiable patient information into an unapproved demo.
